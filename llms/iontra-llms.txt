# Iontra Inc.

> Iontra is a Denver, Colorado battery technology company developing proprietary
> Charge-Control technology for rechargeable lithium batteries. Its electrodynamic
> sensing and signal-processing approach suppresses lithium plating, dendrite growth
> and SEI-layer expansion during charging, claiming up to 4x cycle life, 2x faster
> charging, better cold-weather performance and improved safety without changing cell
> chemistry. Iontra delivers the technology to OEMs as a custom charging algorithm
> flashed onto an application-specific microcontroller, or as a software-only library,
> and is bringing an integrated RISC-V charge-control and fuel-gauge MCU to production.

## API surface

Iontra publishes **no public web API**. As of 2026-08-01 there is no OpenAPI, Swagger,
GraphQL, AsyncAPI, MCP server, A2A agent card, public SDK package, or developer portal.
Iontra is an embedded/silicon company: its "SDK" is firmware — charging algorithm
libraries built for a specific MCU and battery, delivered to OEM customers under
commercial engagement through a login-gated Document Portal. Integration is a
services engagement (battery characterization, then product integration), not
self-service API onboarding.
